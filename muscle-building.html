🏋️ Training & Recovery Guidelines

  • Volume: Perform 3 to 4 sets of 8 to 12 repetitions per exercise for muscle growth.
  • Intensity: Use a weight that makes the final repetitions challenging while maintaining proper form.
  • Rest Between Sets: Rest around 60 to 120 seconds between sets.
  • Recovery: Allow 48 to 72 hours of recovery before training the same muscle group again.
  • Progressive Overload: Gradually increase the weight, repetitions, or training difficulty as you become stronger.
  • Form: Always prioritize correct exercise technique over lifting heavier weights.
