🌱 Healthy Lifestyle Guidelines

  • Balanced Meals: Include a variety of vegetables, fruits, protein sources and whole grains.
  • Protein: Include suitable protein sources such as eggs, dal, dairy, fish, chicken or legumes.
  • Fruits & Vegetables: Eat a variety of colorful fruits and vegetables throughout the week.
  • Hydration: Drink water regularly throughout the day and limit sugary beverages.
  • Healthy Portions: Choose portions that match your energy needs and activity level.
  • Consistency: Focus on healthy habits that you can maintain for the long term rather than extreme diets.
