💪 Muscle Gain Diet Guidelines

  • Protein: Include a protein-rich food in each main meal to support muscle growth and recovery.
  • Calories: Muscle gain generally requires enough total energy to support training and recovery.
  • Carbohydrates: Include nutritious carbohydrate sources such as rice, oats, potatoes and whole grains.
  • Healthy Fats: Include foods such as nuts, seeds, peanuts and other nutritious fat sources.
  • Hydration: Drink enough water throughout the day, especially around workouts.
  • Consistency: Combine a balanced diet with progressive resistance training and adequate recovery.
