🌱 Beginner Training Guidelines

  • Start Slowly: Begin with simple exercises and gradually increase the difficulty.
  • Sets & Repetitions: Start with around 2 to 3 sets of 8 to 12 comfortable repetitions.
  • Warm-Up: Always warm up before exercising to prepare your muscles and joints.
  • Technique: Learn the correct exercise form before increasing weight or intensity.
  • Rest: Take rest days and avoid training through significant pain.
  • Consistency: Focus on building a regular exercise habit rather than trying to progress too quickly.
