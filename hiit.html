🔥 HIIT Training Guidelines

  • Warm-Up: Warm up for around 5 to 10 minutes before starting high-intensity exercises.
  • Work & Rest: Beginners can start with shorter work intervals followed by adequate recovery.
  • Intensity: Perform each exercise with good technique and increase intensity gradually.
  • Duration: A short HIIT session can be effective; avoid unnecessarily long high-intensity workouts.
  • Recovery: Allow sufficient recovery between demanding HIIT sessions.
  • Technique: Stop or reduce intensity if you cannot maintain proper exercise form.
