❤️ Cardio Training Guidelines

  • Warm-Up: Begin with 5 to 10 minutes of light movement before intense cardio.
  • Duration: Start with manageable sessions and gradually increase your workout duration.
  • Intensity: Mix moderate cardio with occasional higher intensity sessions based on your fitness level.
  • Consistency: Regular cardio training is more beneficial than occasional very intense workouts.
  • Hydration: Drink enough water before, during and after your workout.
  • Recovery: Allow your body enough time to recover, especially after demanding cardio sessions.
