🥗 Weight Loss Diet Guidelines

  • Balanced Meals: Include protein, vegetables, whole grains and healthy fats in your meals.
  • Portion Control: Pay attention to portion sizes instead of completely eliminating your favorite foods.
  • Protein: Include a good protein source in your meals to support muscle maintenance and fullness.
  • Fruits & Vegetables: Include a variety of fruits and vegetables throughout the day.
  • Hydration: Drink enough water throughout the day and limit sugary drinks.
  • Consistency: Aim for a sustainable eating pattern rather than extreme dieting.
