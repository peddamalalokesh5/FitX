🔥 Weight Loss Training Guidelines

  • Cardio: Perform around 20 to 40 minutes of moderate cardio depending on your fitness level.
  • HIIT: Start with short intervals and gradually increase intensity as your fitness improves.
  • Strength Training: Include strength exercises regularly to maintain muscle while losing body fat.
  • Daily Activity: Stay active throughout the day by walking, using stairs and reducing long periods of sitting.
  • Nutrition: Weight loss generally requires a sustainable calorie deficit along with adequate protein and nutritious foods.
  • Recovery: Get enough sleep and allow your body to recover between harder training sessions.
