🏋️ Strength Training Guidelines

  • Sets: Perform around 3 to 5 working sets for major strength exercises.
  • Repetitions: Use controlled repetitions and choose a suitable rep range for your training goal.
  • Progressive Overload: Gradually increase weight, repetitions or training difficulty as your strength improves.
  • Rest: Take adequate rest between heavy sets, especially for compound exercises.
  • Technique: Always prioritize proper form and controlled movement instead of lifting excessive weight.
  • Recovery: Give your muscles enough time to recover between demanding training sessions.
